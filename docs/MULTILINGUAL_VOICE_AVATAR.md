# Multilingual Voice & AI Avatar Pipeline

## 1. Supported Languages
- **English** (`en`)
- **Hindi** (`hi`)
- **Hinglish** (`hinglish`)

## 2. Mid-Lesson Dynamic Language Switch
Students can change the language mid-lesson without losing progress or analogy tracking. Natural language intents like *"Ab Hindi me samjhao"* are parsed directly inside the player.

## 3. Voice & Talking Head Avatar
- **ElevenLabs API** for multilingual neural voice synthesis.
- **D-ID & HeyGen APIs** for talking avatar rendering.
- **Graceful Degradation Fallback:** Synced interactive animated audio player with speech waveform and synchronized timestamps so the platform runs flawlessly with or without live third-party credits.
